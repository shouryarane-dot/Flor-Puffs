What is FLŌR?

A world of botanical
snacks to discover.

There are so many interesting ingredients and flavours to discover around the world. Some are familiar in one part of the world, yet new to another. We love exploring these food traditions and bringing them a little closer to home.

FLŌR introduces botanical snacks from different corners of the world. Our first is water lily seed puffs, known as makhana in India.

The product

Water lily seed puffs.

It starts in still water.

Makhana is the seed of the Euryale ferox water lily, traditionally cultivated and enjoyed in India. The seeds are harvested, dried and gently roasted to create the light, crisp puffs at the heart of FLŌR.

A familiar snack tradition in one part of the world, and a discovery for many snack lovers elsewhere.

About us

A little about us.

We are Shourya and Nienke, based in Amsterdam.

Between us, we have spent almost two decades in consumer goods and the retail flower business, industries where quality, packaging and storytelling matter.

We have travelled, lived around different cultures and, like many curious people, tend to come home with things we have eaten. Especially snacks.

FLŌR grew from that curiosity: discovering ingredients and food traditions that deserve to be shared more widely.
