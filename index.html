What is FLŌR

FLŌR is about
food, experienced.

Because food isn't just something to eat. It's something to discover, enjoy and remember.

The taste, the texture, where it comes from, the culture behind it, and the moments it becomes part of. The food we discover while travelling. The things we bring home. The flavours we introduce to someone else. The bowl we put out when the day needs a pause, something to share when friends and family are around, or the quiet ritual of pouring yourself one at the end of a long day.

We discover foods and snacks from different parts of the world and bring them to new tables. Sometimes to share, sometimes simply to enjoy ourselves.

We start with an ingredient familiar in India but new to many elsewhere: makhana, transformed into light, crisp water lily seed puffs.

Water Lily Seed Puffs

It starts
in still water.

A rather unexpected transformation.

Makhana is the seed of Euryale ferox, a water lily traditionally cultivated in India. The seeds are harvested, dried and roasted until they puff into something light, airy and crisp.

Makhana has been eaten in India for generations. We wanted to bring it somewhere new, and let the ingredient speak for itself.

Light & crisp. FLŌR Puffs are seasoned with the same attention we give everything we make: enough to make them interesting, never so much that you lose the ingredient underneath.

Sustainability

Not a box
to tick.

Sustainability isn't something we're adding to FLŌR because we're supposed to. It's a must-have for us. Our makhana is sourced through a trader today, and we're exploring ways to source more directly and pay farmers a meaningful premium. Our pouch isn't perfect either. Protecting crispness means a multi-layer pack, and our ambition is a mono-material, recyclable one. We're still at the beginning, and honest about what we haven't figured out yet.
