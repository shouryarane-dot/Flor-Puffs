The Seed

Grown on water.
Finished by hand.

Makhana — the seed of the water lily — has been gathered from still ponds for centuries. It is drawn up by hand, dried in the sun, and puffed over an open flame until it opens into something impossibly light.

There is no extrusion, no shaping, no filler. What you hold is the seed itself, expanded. We season it sparingly, because the point was never to cover it up.

FLŌR exists for the moments in between — the pause you take rather than the meal you plan.
